How it works

Three steps, and the third is optional.

  1. A reminder appears

    Once a day, MedRemind shows a reminder on your phone. With Plus you get three slots: morning, noon and evening.

  2. You press the button

    One large button, easy to see and easy to hit. A 24-hour countdown then shows when the next reminder is due.

  3. Family gets a short emailOptional

    Add a family member’s or caregiver’s email address and they get a confirmation each time you press. Leave it empty and no email is ever sent.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Does MedRemind alert anyone if a check-in is missed?

No. MedRemind does not send an automatic alert when a check-in is missed. An email is only sent when the button is pressed, and only if an email address has been added. It is a voluntary daily check-in, not a safety alarm.

Does it verify that medication was taken?

No. A button press only means the button was pressed. MedRemind does not verify health, safety or medication intake, and it is not medical monitoring.

Does the person who gets the email need the app?

No. They only need an email address. There is no account or password on either side.

What happens if I do not add an email address?

Then no email is sent. The app works the same: a reminder, one button, and the countdown to the next reminder.

How much does Plus cost?

Plus is a separate paid Android edition: a 36 SEK one-time purchase on Google Play. No subscription and no recurring fees. Google Play shows the current price in your country.

How does Plus differ from Free?

Free covers the simple daily check-in and may show ads. Plus removes the ads and adds three reminder slots — morning, noon and evening.

Do you sell my data?

No. Your settings are stored on your phone and no account is required. The optional confirmation email is delivered through an external email provider, and Free may show Google AdMob ads. The Privacy Policy describes the exact data flow.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. MedRemind is Android first; iOS is on the roadmap.

Which languages does it support?

English, Swedish, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese.
