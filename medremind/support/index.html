Support

Help with MedRemind.

MedRemind is made and supported by one person. Write to contact@maxineplatform.com and you will get a reply from a human, normally within a day or two.

MedRemind is a reminder and check-in tool. It is not an emergency service or a medical device, and it does not alert anyone if a check-in is missed. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

When you write, it helps to include

  • Whether you use MedRemind Free or MedRemind Plus.
  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

Common questions

The reminder does not appear

Check that notifications are allowed for MedRemind in your phone's settings. Some phones also stop apps in the background to save battery; if reminders arrive late or not at all, exclude MedRemind from battery optimisation. Reminders can also be missed if the phone is switched off or out of power.

The family email does not arrive

Open the app's settings and check the email address for typing mistakes. Then ask the recipient to look in their spam or junk folder: the email is sent from noreply@maxineplatform.com. An email is only sent when the button is pressed, and only if an address has been saved.

I want to stop the emails

Remove the email address in the app's settings. With no address saved, no email is sent.

How do I delete my data?

Your name, email address and settings are stored on your phone. Clearing the app's data in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes them. See the MedRemind Privacy Policy for the full data flow.

Refunds for MedRemind Plus

Plus is a one-time purchase made through Google Play, and payments and refunds are handled under Google Play's terms. If something went wrong with a purchase, write to me as well and I will help where I can.

Change the language

Tap the globe icon at the top of the app. MedRemind is available in English, Swedish, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese.
