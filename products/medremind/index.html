FAQ

How much does Plus cost? +

Plus is available now on Google Play — a separate paid Android edition for a 36 SEK one-time purchase. No subscription, no recurring fees.

Where can I get the Free app? +

Free is available now on Google Play — search for MedRemind or use the Get it on Google Play button above.

Does MedRemind verify that medication was taken, or alert anyone if a check-in is missed? +

No. A button press only means the button was pressed. MedRemind does not verify health, safety, or medication intake, and it does not send an automatic alert if a check-in is missed. It is a voluntary daily check-in, not medical monitoring or a safety alarm.

Do you sell my data? +

We do not use Maxine-owned analytics or data brokers. Reminder settings are intended to stay on the device. Optional email confirmation uses an external email provider when configured, and Free may show Google AdMob ads. See the Privacy Policy for the exact data flow.

Is it available on iPhone? +

Android first. iOS is on the roadmap.

Is it available in my language? +

We currently ship in English, Swedish, Spanish, German, French, Italian, and Portuguese.

How does Plus differ from Free? +

Free covers the simple daily check-in flow and may show ads. Plus is a separate paid Android edition that removes ads and adds more reminder slots. It is not a subscription.
